## Overview

Built an encoder-decoder LSTM chatbot with embeddings and attention mechanisms to support multi-turn conversations.

## Training

- Prepared and augmented conversational datasets.
- Used teacher forcing, gradient clipping, and early stopping during training.
- Applied beam search decoding to improve response quality and multi-turn context handling.

## Technologies

Python, deep learning, sequence-to-sequence modeling, and LSTM networks.
